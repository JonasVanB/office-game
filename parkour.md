# Rooftop parkour – layout and build notes

An outdoor time-trial course around and on top of our building ([parkour.js](parkour.js)): a full loop over the neighbour's roof, along both long facades, over the flat roof twice and along both curved-roof ridges. The movement is first-person, fluid and built on momentum: wall runs, vaults, ledge grabs, wall climbs, slides, rolls and a zipline. **Yellow means touch this:** every ledge, wall-run panel, climb strip, obstacle top and the zipline on the route is yellow. Everything around it is grey, white or concrete.

Coordinates follow [layout.md](layout.md): x 0 → 50 along the building, z 0 (side A) → 13 (side B), y = 0 is the 4e floor. The outer walls stand at x −0.25 / 50.25 and z −0.25 / 13.25.

## The route

Start at the **east** emergency door, run over the neighbour's roof, go west along the side A facade, climb onto our roof, cross it, go up onto the west curved roof and run its ridge. Slide down the side B slope and drop onto a scaffold deck, run east along the side B scaffolding, climb back onto the roof, cross to the east curved roof, run its ridge and take the zipline back over the start to the finish on the neighbour's roof. From there E takes you back inside through the east door.

⚑ = checkpoint (yellow lamp, green once passed). Checkpoints count only in order, so a shortcut that skips one (for example climbing onto the roof from the deck) cannot finish. Falling below y −4.5 fades the screen and puts you back at the last checkpoint. The timer keeps running and the fall is counted.

**Times:** a scripted, perfect-input run takes **22.3 s** (splits 5.3 / 8.8 / 10.3 / 12.7 / 15.3 / 18.8 s). Expect about 35–60 s for a good human run, and a couple of minutes for a first try.

## Movement

| Move | Input | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Run | W | Momentum: **7.0 m/s** quickly, then up to **9.4 m/s** after about 1.7 s of running straight. Walls, hard landings and stopping reset it. Shift = walk |
| Jump | Space | 5.7 m/s up. Holding Space jumps higher (lighter gravity going up). **Coyote time** 0.12 s, **jump buffer** 0.15 s. Low air control, so momentum carries you |
| Vault | automatic when running (or Space) | Obstacles 0.42–1.3 m high: over it if it is ≤ 1.9 m deep, otherwise a quick hop onto it. Keeps ~97% of your speed. Also works from a standstill when you push forward against it |
| Ledge grab + mantle | automatic | An edge 0.5–2.35 m above your feet, in front of you, while in the air: grab and pull up (0.2 s + 0.1 s per metre) |
| Wall run | jump alongside a wall, holding W | At ≥ 3.8 m/s and mostly parallel to the wall: a short arc (about 0.5 m up, max 1.3 s, at least 7.5 m/s) with the camera tilted. Space kicks off at an angle. Something low ahead on the wall run: you vault onto it; anything else in the way drops you off the wall |
| Wall climb | Space while running at a tall wall (or run into it in the air holding W) | About 2 m up the wall. A ledge in reach is grabbed. Space during the climb turns you around and kicks you off |
| Slide | C while running | Low and fast (up to 11 m/s) under 0.95 m gaps. Keeps going while you hold C, and speeds up on the curved roofs |
| Roll | C just before a big landing | Landing from more than ~2 m: with C you roll and keep your speed. Without a roll, a landing at more than 12.5 m/s staggers you |
| Zipline | jump or run off into the cable | Snaps on within ~1 m of the cable; brakes at the end. Space lets go |
| Respawn | R | Back to the last checkpoint |
| **Full reset** | **Backspace** (or hold R, or the ↺ button on touch) | Straight back to the start line with the clock cleared. The HUD shows "R checkpoint · Backspace full reset" under the timer |
| Leave | E | On the balcony or after the finish: back inside through the east door |

**Feel:** the FOV widens with speed (up to +15°), with head bob and footsteps in step, a landing dip that scales with the impact, camera roll on wall runs, a dip on vaults and mantles, a smooth turn on a climb kick-off, speed lines, wind noise that grows with speed, and a zipline trolley sound. The sounds live in sound.js: pkStep, pkLand, pkGrab, pkSlide, pkCheck, pkFinish, plus the wind and zip loops.

**Touch:** left half = move stick, right half = look, plus Jump (hold for higher), Slide and ↺ (full reset) buttons.

## Leaderboard, ghost and achievements

- **Shared:** `parkour2/<account id>` = {t (ms), n, s (splits), d}, through `G.mp.parkour` in mp.js. Firebase only accepts 15 s – 30 min and never a slower time than the one stored (database.rules.json). Local test mode keeps it in localStorage (`office.localParkour2.<id>`). The old, shorter course used `parkour/`: those times are no longer read (delete the node in the Firebase console if you want it gone for good)
- **In the world:** leaderboards on the east wall by the balcony and on the plant room next to the finish pad (top 10, your row highlighted)
- **HUD:** time, checkpoint count, best time and speed. Each checkpoint flashes your split versus your PB (green −, red +)
- **Ghost:** your best run is recorded every 0.1 s (localStorage `office.parkour2`; the old `office.parkour` is removed) and replays as a translucent runner
- **Achievements (progress.js, "Parkour", ids `pk_*`):** Free runner (step out), Rooftop run (finish), Quick feet (< 45 s), Flow state (< 30 s), No safety net (no falls), Beat the ghost (new PB), King of the roof (#1), Wall runner (50 wall runs)

## Build

- `parkour.js` builds the course and has its own 3D physics. Solids are axis-aligned boxes plus the curved roofs as a height function (`roofF`). It takes `GAME.controlLock` while you are outside, sets the camera itself (roll, FOV) and reports your feet height through `GAME.feetY`
- `index.html` hooks: `GAME.feetY` (shared y + no stair/level switching while outside) and `GAME.zoneOverride` ("Rooftop parkour"). The stairwell window in wall A (x 30–34.65, behind the first wall-run panel) has an indoor collider on the 4e and 3e, so you cannot walk out through it
- Console helpers: `PARKOUR.enter()`, `PARKOUR.go(n)` (jump to checkpoint n), `PARKOUR.restart()`, `PARKOUR.me`
